For grown-ups

  1. Feel It first. Two minutes a day for a week. A child needs to feel ten seconds and one minute before clock numbers mean anything.
  2. Time Pie for one real job a day. Let her pick the job and press Start. The timer is the boss, so you do not have to be.
  3. Clock Quest one level at a time. Stay on a level until she gets three stars twice. Short hand only comes first on purpose.
  4. Mission is hers to run. She taps Done. You stay quiet and do not remind. Praise finishing on her own, not being fast.
  5. Set the jobs with her. Time each job once on a calm day and use those minutes below, plus a little extra.
